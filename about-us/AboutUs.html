    Who We Are

    A Business Model Purpose-Built for Commercial Kitchens

    In the commercial poultry sector, success is measured not in consumer retail promises, but in consistency: weight uniformity, strict cold preservation, and uncompromised morning delivery punctuality.

    Thangam Farms was established to eliminate the unpredictable variances that executive chefs and purchasing directors often encounter when sourcing from fragmented retail markets. By controlling our supply chain from farm sourcing to final cold-chain dispatch at Kannampalayam, we deliver exact carcass weights and pure, chemical-free freshness day after day.

    Our commercial relationships are built around direct communication, transparent business practices, and tailored cutting protocols that respect the kitchen economics of our clients.

    Partnerships Built Around Kitchen Realities

    Every commercial food establishment has specific requirements: a 5-star hotel kitchen needs calibrated 1.8kg whole birds for buffet roasts; a cloud kitchen network requires uniform 250g skinless breasts with zero bone fragments; a wholesale butcher needs live weight yield consistency.

    At Thangam Farms, our wholesale relationships begin with an operational assessment of your kitchen’s cutting standards, daily volume requirements, and receiving bay access times. We then establish a customized dispatch schedule to ensure your team never deals with unexpected substitutions or compromised delivery windows.

    How We Structure B2B Engagements

    1

    Specification Assessment: We document your exact portion weights, packaging preferences (crats vs vacuum wrap), and delivery schedules.

    2

    Trial Dispatch Verification: A calibrated sample batch is processed at Kannampalayam for your culinary team to inspect for yield and cleanliness.

    3

    Formal Supply Agreement: A dedicated commercial supply allocation is set up with prioritized daily dawn delivery.
