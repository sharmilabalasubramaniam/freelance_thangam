    Quality Assurance, Hygiene & Responsible Supply

    A comprehensive look at our cold-chain maintenance, hygienic handling disciplines, and institutional food safety practices at our Kannampalayam processing unit.

    Hygienic Discipline

    Freshness Rooted in Methodical Handling

    In high-volume commercial food service, hygiene is not a marketing phrase—it is an uncompromising operational parameter. A single deviation in holding temperature or surface sanitation can spoil hundreds of kilograms of poultry and endanger customer trust.

    At Thangam Farms, our handling procedures follow four non-negotiable principles: pure water washing, continuous low-temperature staging, strict knife-and-board sterilization, and unbroken refrigerated transfer directly to hotel and restaurant receiving bays.

    We operate with complete procedural transparency, welcoming our commercial clients' executive chefs and quality inspectors to review our facility layout, chilling tanks, and hygiene logs.

    Systematic Protocols

    The 4 Operational Benchmarks of STP Chickens

    Every bird moving through our Kannampalayam center is handled under these four documented benchmarks.

    Protocol 01 thermostat

    Freshness-Focused Handling

    Immediately following mechanical dressing, broilers pass through sanitized pre-chilling water bath systems followed by direct blast staging between 0°C and 4°C. Rapid temperature reduction inhibits microbial multiplication and locks in natural carcass moisture without the need for artificial chemical retainers.

    Chilling Threshold: 0°C to 4°C Active Cold Staging
    Protocol 02 sanitizer

    Clean Handling Practices

    All food-contact surfaces, including stainless steel cutting benches, dressing troughs, and portioning blades, undergo a strict three-stage sanitization cycle (pre-rinse, food-grade detergent scrub, high-pressure hot water thermal flush) before and after each morning shift. Hand-washing and protective apparel are mandatory across all processing zones.

    Surface Standard: SS 304 Grade Shift Disinfection
    Protocol 03 balance

    Quality Consistency & Grading

    Uniformity is essential for commercial recipe profitability. Each bird is manually inspected on illuminated grading tables to weed out skin tears, bruising, or anomalous discoloration. Birds are precisely sorted into tight weight brackets (e.g., 1.6kg–1.8kg or 1.8kg–2.0kg) ensuring uniform cook times in commercial tandoors and rotisseries.

    Inspection Standard: 100% Visual Screen Strict Weight Grading
    Protocol 04 local_shipping

    Responsible Supply Operations

    We work exclusively with ethical broiler growers adhering to clean grain-based feed regimens and humane handling standards. Our dispatch logistics minimize fuel waste through optimized dawn routing schedules, ensuring birds spend minimal time in transit between our Kannampalayam hub and your storage bays.

    Logistics Focus: Direct Transit Hub Low-Transit Duration
