# STP CHICKENS — Customer CRM & Lead Management Guide

## 1. CRM Overview

The STP CHICKENS Lead Management module in the Admin Portal tracks wholesale poultry procurement requests from commercial kitchens, hotels, catering companies, and retail distributors.

---

## 2. Lead Status Pipeline

| Status | Stage Meaning | Staff Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **New** | Just submitted by client or AI assistant | Review within 2 hours; verify requirements |
| **Contacted** | Staff has phoned or messaged the client | Confirm daily volume, cut specifications & drop address |
| **In Progress** | Quote shared, contract/sample pending | Schedule logistics route and delivery timeline |
| **Completed** | Ongoing wholesale customer contract | Move to active dispatch scheduling |
| **Cancelled** | Order below 50 kg MOQ or outside service area | Note reason in internal admin notes |

---

## 3. CRM Operations in Admin Dashboard

1. **Search & Filter:**
   - Filter by status dropdown (*New, Contacted, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled*).
   - Search by customer name, business entity, phone, or enquiry ID.
   - Filter by submission date.

2. **Customer Detail Drawer:**
   - **One-Click Call Link:** Opens phone dialer directly to client (`tel:+91...`).
   - **One-Click WhatsApp Link:** Opens prefilled WhatsApp chat with the customer's enquiry reference number.
   - **Internal Notes:** Staff can record specific instructions (e.g. *"Requires 16-piece biryani cut at 05:00 AM delivery"*).

3. **Secure CSV Export:**
   - Click **Export CSV** in the toolbar to download full enquiry records for external accounting or logistics analysis.
